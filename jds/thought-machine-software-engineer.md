# Software Engineer

## About the Role

Thought Machine's mission is to properly and permanently rid the world's banks of legacy technology. To achieve this, the company has developed cloud-native core banking and payments technology.

Thought Machine has grown to more than 550 people across offices in London, New York, Singapore, Sydney, and its Engineering Hub in Lisbon. It has raised more than £500m in funding. Its investors include Molten Ventures, Eurazeo, Intesa Sanpaolo, Temasek, Nyca Partners, JPMorgan Chase Strategic Investments, and Standard Chartered Ventures.

Software Engineering is a key role within Thought Machine. Back End Engineers lead the development of the Vault product, adopting continuous deployment and monorepo-style development practices. Development is fast-paced, and engineers are expected to produce code to a high, production-ready standard.

## Responsibilities

- Design, implement, and develop scalable, performant microservices using best practices.
- Write automated unit and integration tests as part of the development cycle.
- Work with other engineering teams to ensure features are added in a structured and coherent way.
- Manage and debug deployments from testing environments through to production.
- Translate customer requirements into trackable tickets.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Experience with either Python or Golang.
- Experience developing automated tests as an integral part of the development cycle.
- Interest in client-facing projects, including conducting scoping and defining deliverables.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Knowledge of banking or finance.
- Experience with AWS or other cloud providers.
- Familiarity with SQL or NoSQL databases.
- Experience with client/server software architectures and networking, or microservice architectures.
- Experience using orchestration tools such as Kubernetes or Mesos.
