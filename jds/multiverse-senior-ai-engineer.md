# Senior AI Engineer - AI Transformation

## About the Role

Multiverse is the upskilling platform for AI and Tech adoption. It has partnered with 1,500+ companies to deliver learning designed to help people at any age and career stage build critical AI, data, and tech skills. Multiverse says its learners have driven $2bn+ ROI for their employers through improved productivity and measurable performance.

In April 2026, Multiverse announced $70 million in strategic funding led by Schroders Capital, with participation from StepStone Group, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and General Catalyst. At a stated valuation of $2.1bn, the company describes itself as Europe's first EdTech double unicorn. It has more than 800 employees and plans to continue scaling.

The AI Transformation team is a small, focused squad driving Multiverse's next wave of growth. Its mandate is to rebuild how the company works, function by function, and establish the practices that make Multiverse an AI-first company. The team is also building a product it intends to take to market as part of becoming a transformation partner to its customers.

The team is high velocity, cross-functional, and broad in scope. It works alongside colleagues whose work it is reimagining and with the wider engineering organisation responsible for Multiverse's customer-facing product. The structure is described as flat and fast, with a bias to action and fun at its core.

## Responsibilities

- Own and deliver end-to-end AI systems, including agents and the platforms they depend on. Identify user problems, break them down, and build AI systems that solve them in close partnership with the teams that will adopt them.
- Design context and retrieval strategies, including retrieval pipelines, conversation memory, summarisation strategies, chunking logic, and role-based access control (RBAC), so context is useful and permission-aware.
- Build automated evaluation pipelines and human-in-the-loop review processes that show whether AI systems are performing as intended.
- Design and build the tool layer for agents, including MCPs, APIs, data contracts, and error handling that makes tool use reliable.
- Influence technical direction by contributing evidence-backed opinions to architectural decisions, challenging unsuitable approaches, and proposing better ones.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Experience shipping production AI systems that serve real users at scale.
- Understanding of the engineering challenges that distinguish agent systems from conventional software, including context management, model selection and routing, cost engineering, tool use, agent augmentation, and evaluation.
- A product mindset: identify real user pain, understand requirements, build pragmatic and high-impact solutions, and test, learn, and iterate quickly under high ambiguity.
- Full-stack delivery experience spanning LLM integration, backend services, data pipelines, and enough frontend development to ship end to end.
- Ability to explain technical concepts clearly to engineers and business colleagues, proactively engage stakeholders, and bring them along on the journey.
- Fluency in AI-assisted development and the ability to mentor others to use it well.
