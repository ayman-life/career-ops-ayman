# Product Engineer

## About the Role

Attio is building a CRM for agentic revenue teams, enabling companies to understand customers, automate workflows at scale, and tailor their go-to-market operations. The company has raised $116M from investors including GV, Redpoint, Balderton, Point Nine, and 01A.

The Engineering team works across real-time infrastructure, AI-native architecture, and a high-performance frontend. Attio is hiring a Product Engineer to build polished, performant systems and user experiences for a data-rich application.

## Responsibilities

- Build product features across Attio's Node.js backend and React frontend using JavaScript and TypeScript.
- Deliver advanced user experiences in a high-performance, data-rich application.
- Apply modern frontend-development or distributed-systems expertise to product problems.
- Learn from and collaborate with experienced technical colleagues.
- Represent Attio's Product and Engineering values internally and externally.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Expertise in JavaScript and TypeScript.
- A record of independently delivering solutions to real customer or product problems while working as part of a team.
- A proactive and self-motivated approach to problem solving.
- A strong interest in learning new skills and experimenting with new techniques.
- An understanding of how strong technical execution supports excellent user experiences.
- The ability to explain technical ideas and influence stakeholders across Engineering, Product, and Design.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience building backends with Node.js.
- Experience building frontends with React or a comparable framework.
- Experience contributing to or participating in open-source projects.
- Experience with data-rich applications or large volumes of real-time data.
- Enthusiasm for experimenting with new ideas, tools, techniques, and technologies.

## Our Stack

- JavaScript / TypeScript
- Node.js
- React
- Modern frontend technologies
- Distributed systems
- Real-time data infrastructure

## What We Value

- Strong judgement and technical craft.
- High standards for quality and attention to detail.
- Care for the people who use the product.
- Comfort with complex technical challenges.
- Fast, rigorous execution.
- Curiosity and continual learning.
