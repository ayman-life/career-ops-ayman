# Senior AI Engineer

## About the Role

Healthtech-1 is building technology intended to improve access to NHS healthcare. Its goal is to make NHS appointments transparent and instantly bookable while using AI to guide patients to the right service first time.

The company says its patient-registration software is used by 26% of GP practices in England, while one in five GP practices in London use Bookable Search to help patients find and book appointments. Its newer product, Bookable Navigator, automatically books appointments for existing patients.

Healthtech-1 operates from within a working GP practice in East London and is backed by Y Combinator, Innovate UK, and the NHS Clinical Entrepreneur Programme. It says it was previously ranked the eighth fastest-growing startup in the UK and the 33rd fastest-growing in Europe.

The company is hiring a Senior AI Engineer who has built and deployed real AI products, measured and monitored their effectiveness, iterated on their implementation, and launched them to users who depend on accurate results. The central challenge is developing clinically capable AI for high-stakes care-navigation scenarios, with appropriate training, context, and defensible decisions.

The engineer will own and shape patient- and clinician-facing experiences where performance, clarity, and reliability directly affect real patients.

## Responsibilities

- Own end-to-end product features across frontend, backend, data, and API layers through to production.
- Build AI-powered care-navigation experiences.
- Integrate model providers and AI tooling into dependable product experiences.
- Develop evaluation and feedback systems that make AI behaviour measurable and improvable over time.
- Build trust surfaces that support certification as a medical device and national procurement.
- Work closely with founders, customers, and domain experts to turn ambiguity into shipped product.
- Make pragmatic product and technical decisions without waiting for perfect requirements.
- Use automation, AI-assisted development, and internal tooling to increase engineering velocity.

## Minimum Qualifications

- At least five years of professional engineering experience, with broad full-stack range.
- Demonstrable experience shipping AI features that real users rely on.
- Ability to discuss prompts, evaluations, failure modes, latency, cost, and the UX trade-offs involved in building with language models.
- Fluency with AI coding tools and well-founded opinions about where they help and where they fall short.
- Experience in an environment with a high engineering bar, such as FAANG, a leading AI lab, or a respected product-engineering organisation.
- Desire for greater ownership, speed, and upside than larger or more established environments typically offer.
- Strong product judgement and the ability to make pragmatic decisions under ambiguity.

## Preferred Qualifications
