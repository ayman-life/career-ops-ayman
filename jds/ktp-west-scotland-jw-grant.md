# KTP Associate – AI Software Engineering

## About the Role

This 33-month Knowledge Transfer Partnership brings together the University of the West of Scotland (UWS), Innovate UK, and J W Grant & Son Ltd. Working closely with academic supervisors and J W Grant stakeholders, the KTP Associate will design, develop, and deploy AI capabilities that support the company's long-term growth and sustainability.

The role applies full-stack AI engineering—including large language models, agentic AI, and emerging Model Context Protocol (MCP) approaches—to connect data and workflows across sales and service, stock management, and supply-chain operations. The Associate will deliver secure, compliant, user-focused solutions that produce measurable efficiency gains while balancing academic rigour with commercial pragmatism.

The project sits at the centre of J W Grant's growth strategy. The Associate will work directly with directors and branch teams, using live trading data and real commercial problems to take solutions from idea through deployment.

## Responsibilities

- Curate data and develop databases and pipelines for large language model and agentic AI systems.
- Develop AI solutions integrated across multiple real-world systems and data sources.
- Develop, test, and deploy AI models for monitoring and optimising J W Grant operations.
- Integrate data and workflows supporting sales, customer service, stock, and supply-chain management.
- Deliver secure, reliable, scalable, compliant, and user-focused AI solutions.
- Manage the project lifecycle and work within an agile team to meet business requirements.
- Prepare technical and non-technical reports, presentations, whitepapers, and case studies.
- Communicate effectively with academic supervisors, company stakeholders, and team members.
- Engage, train, and support staff through organisational change, building trust in AI through clear communication and demonstrable value.
- Disseminate project activity through academic journals and conferences.

## Minimum Qualifications

- An MSc or MEng in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Programming experience in one or more languages such as Python, C++, C#, Java, or JavaScript.
- Experience using large language models and open-source AI libraries and frameworks.
- Experience with database systems and integrations, such as MySQL or NoSQL databases.
- Experience in technical writing and preparing reports and documentation.

## Preferred Qualifications

- A PhD in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Experience integrating LLMs into applications using APIs, prompt engineering, and fine-tuning.
- Experience with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and/or foundational agentic AI projects, such as Model Context Protocol (MCP) and AGENTS.md.

## Our Stack
